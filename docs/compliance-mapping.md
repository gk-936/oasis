# Compliance mapping: design alignment, NOT compliance

**Read this first.** Oasis is a hackathon sandbox. It holds **no licence** in India, China or anywhere else, has **no regulator sign-off**, performs **no real settlement** (all money, rails, registry identities and balances are simulated and labelled MOCK), and has had **no independent audit, penetration test or certification**. The table below maps controls that exist in this repository to obligations and frameworks they resemble, so a reviewer can see what the design points toward. It does **not** claim that Oasis complies with, satisfies or is certified under any law or standard. It is not legal advice. Whether any Oasis activity needs a licence, and how any statute applies to a real deployment, is a question for counsel (see [regulatory-sources.md](regulatory-sources.md), "Questions only an authorised dealer bank / counsel can answer").

How to read a row: **Control** is what the code does; **Code / doc** is where; **Test** is the automated test that exercises it (names from the repo and from [security-evidence-matrix.md](security-evidence-matrix.md); run with `PYTHONPATH=. .venv/Scripts/python -m pytest -q <path::name>`); **Not covered** is what the control does not do. Regulatory figures behind rows 1 to 3 were researched on 2026-10-11, several from secondary sources, and are not confirmed by a bank or counsel; each rule carries its source and status in `oasis/trust/rules/inr_cny.yaml`, in the Passport tab card "Corridor rules & sources" and at `GET /api/corridor/rules`.

## What this mapping deliberately does not say

- It does not say Oasis is compliant, certified, approved, or safe to use with real money.
- It does not say any legal figure here is final: the TCS rates in particular come from tax portals, a statute mirror and a bank notice, because the official page could not be read.
- Controls that exist are sandbox controls. Closing the "Not covered" column would need an authorised dealer bank or other licensed partner, counsel, and an independent security review.
