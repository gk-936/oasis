**Exchange rates (the one real market input).** With `OASIS_RATES=live` (the Docker image's default) the INR→CNY mid-market rate is the real ECB euro foreign-exchange reference cross rate, and the wallet's USD, EUR, GBP and AUD crosses come from the same response, (CNY per EUR / INR per EUR) via Frankfurter, with jsDelivr (fawazahmed0) as a secondary source, cached for 1 hour. If both fail, a stale cached rate is shown amber (STALE), else the mock. Route spreads, fees and delivery times stay MOCK on top of the live mid-market rate, a reference rate is not a rate anyone will actually execute at, and Oasis never predicts rates. Locally the default, and always the scripted demo, the benchmark and the tests, use the mock, whose base (0.0692) is a frozen snapshot of the real level as of 2026-10-09 (ECB via Frankfurter), not a live value. Details: [live-rates.md](live-rates.md).

Not built: real banking integration, a multi-host FISCO network, WeIdentity, the Python app serving TLS itself (hybrid post-quantum TLS is tested at a Caddy reverse proxy with a classical certificate, local only, browsers not tested: [pq-tls.md](pq-tls.md), `eval/reports/pqtls.md`), a local offline language model or OCR (the deterministic parser is the offline path). (The SQLite payload columns are AES-256-GCM encrypted; table names, row ids and counts are not, and the key files are plaintext unless `OASIS_KEY_PASSPHRASE` is set.)

No result is a production claim. Corridor figures are "as of October 2026" and each still needs its official confirmation step (see `oasis/trust/rules/inr_cny.yaml`).
