# Informal interviews

Copied into `Evidence/interviews.md` by `eval/build_evidence_folder.py`.

- **Who:** 10 people we know. 6 are international students and interns abroad (Vietnam, Japan, Germany, Spain and Tanzania) and 4 are parents.
- **What we asked:** their experience paying tuition and rent abroad, sending money across borders, fees, scams or fake payees, and building a credit history abroad.
- **What we heard** (individual accounts, not measured frequencies):
  - 2 of the 6 students met a fake landlord while looking for a home. Neither was scammed, because the landlord refused a live viewing and offered only pictures, and when someone was sent to check in person the house did not exist.
  - 1 student had trouble opening a bank account abroad, which delayed rent and other payments.
  - 3 of the 4 parents could not send money because the exchange-rate steps were involved, and some did not understand them.
  - 3 interning students found the difference between exchange rates high when spending.
- **Limits:** a small, informal, convenience sample of people we know. It is not representative and it is not a survey. Finance offices were not interviewed.
